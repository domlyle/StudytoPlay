Backup and restore

Save everything to one .json file: decks, cards and pictures, weak spots, points, rank and study log. Use it as a safety copy or to move to another device. Your AI keys are never included.

Restore replaces everything here with the backup. Merge adds missing decks, cards, weak spots and study sessions, and keeps your current points, rank and streak.

Profile and sync

Sign in to keep your decks, pictures, points, rank and study log in sync across your devices. Without signing in, everything stays only on this device.

Signed in

Syncs automatically while you use the app. If two devices change things at the same time, decks, cards, weak spots and study sessions are combined and points are added together. A card deleted on one device can come back if the other device edited at the same time. Your AI keys and the running stopwatch are never synced.
